  • PreparationStudent

    Decide on Calculus and Probability Online

    UniMelb's online maths bridging subject, covering similar ground to VCE Mathematical Methods 3/4. A pass (50%+) meets the maths prerequisite for Biomedicine, Science, Commerce and Agriculture. That's a formal box ticked, not a reason to drop maths in Year 13: first-year science everywhere assumes it, and Otago strongly recommends Level 3 Calculus. It gives no credit towards a degree. It assumes VCE Methods 1/2 (roughly NCEA Level 2 Maths: algebra, functions and graphs, intro calculus). Two modules: Calculus (functions, limits, differentiation, integration; A$660) and Probability (sets, counting, conditional probability, tree diagrams; A$330), A$990 for both. Summer workload: 23–30 hours a week for both modules. Which module(s) count depends on prior study, and UniMelb recommends both if unsure, so email the NCEA maths record to online-calcprob@unimelb.edu.au and ask before registering. Summer results can't be used for entry that same year (2027), but they're in time for a 2028 start. Working plan: the Calculus module only, with probability covered by Level 2 Probability and Level 3 Statistics in Year 13, if UniMelb agrees (asked by email). UniMelb is the strict one. Sydney's medical science degrees (USyd, UNSW, UTS, Macquarie, Western Sydney) list maths only as assumed knowledge. NZ biomedical degrees (Otago, Auckland, Victoria, Canterbury) need only University Entrance, though Otago strongly recommends Level 3 Calculus. Monash keeps its own list of alternatives. RMIT and Swinburne fashion have no maths prerequisite. Course page · Overview video · Monash maths prerequisites

  • PreparationStudent

    Calculus and Probability summer intensive starts

    6 teaching weeks, to Fri 12 Feb. Each week: watch the lecture videos, submit the weekly questions and join the online Zoom consultations (needs a webcam). UniMelb's estimate for Summer is 23–30 hours a week for both modules, 16–20 for Calculus only and 7–10 for Probability only. That's close to a full-time job for 6 weeks; the overview video's 8–12 hours is for the longer offerings. Each week's material is released at the start of that week, so work can't be done ahead. Needs a computer and reliable internet every week, so plan summer trips around it. Tutorial times are in Melbourne time; add 2 hours for NZ (both are on daylight saving until 4 Apr). Overview video

    Year 13 starts — keep English and Chemistry

    English for the English prerequisite (Swinburne, UniMelb); Chemistry for UniMelb Biomedicine. Schools start between 28 Jan and 3 Feb. So a failed bridging course is retaken (Autumn or Spring 2027), not covered at school. MoE term dates

    Calculus and Probability exam (online)

    Monday, sat online via Zoom with an invigilator, in week 4 of NZ Term 1. Writing time is 1.5 hours for one module or 3 hours for both, plus 15 minutes' reading.
