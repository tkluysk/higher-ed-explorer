### Admission and NCEA

2. **NCEA assessment changes from 2027 entry.** Up to 2026 entry, UniMelb converted NCEA itself (best 80 Level 3 credits). From 2027 entry it will use the converted ATAR that VTAC receives. Subject prerequisites are unchanged. *[00:43–01:52]*
   - NZQA calculates an ATAR for eligible NZ students in January, from the **best 90 Level 3 credits, max 24 per subject**. This matches the "90" the staff member quoted. — [NZQA: Study at Australian universities with NCEA](https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/international/nz-quals-overseas/study-with-ncea/australia/)
   - UniMelb's current NCEA pages still describe the old method; staff said they're being updated. — [UniMelb: How you're selected (NCEA)](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/how-to-apply/undergraduate-study/domestic-applications/entry-requirements/equivalent-qualifications-to-the-vce/how-youre-selected-applicants-with-ncea) · [UniMelb: NCEA entry scores](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/how-to-apply/undergraduate-study/recognised-vce-equivalent-qualifications/qualifications/new-zealand-national-certificate-in-educational-achievement-ncea)
   - VTAC's guide for NZ applicants: [VTAC quick start: Interstate and NZ Year 12](https://vtac.edu.au/guides/quickstart/interstate-nz) · [VTAC: Supplying results from Australia or NZ](https://vtac.edu.au/evidence/ausnzresults)
3. **NZ online information sessions** run around April–May. Register for course updates. *[03:01]* — [UniMelb: Information for schools — Australia and NZ](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/connect-with-us/information-for-schools/Australia-and-New-Zealand)

   - [VTAC key dates](https://vtac.edu.au/dates.html) · [UniMelb: Key information for VTAC applicants](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/study-with-us/undergraduate-courses/change-of-preference/key-information-VTAC-applicants) · [UniMelb: Access Melbourne — how to apply](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/how-to-apply/equity-entry-schemes/access-melbourne-undergraduate/how-to-apply) (the equity/scholarships application closes early October; late applications are not accepted)

### Courses and prerequisites

5. **Bachelor of Biomedicine prerequisites: English, Mathematics (NCEA Mathematics with Calculus) and Chemistry**, all mandatory. *[12:52–13:12]* **Bachelor of Science** accepts any one of Biology, Chemistry or Physics as the science subject. *[16:03–16:24]*
   - [Biomedicine entry requirements](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/find/courses/undergraduate/bachelor-of-biomedicine/entry-requirements/) · [Science entry requirements](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/find/courses/undergraduate/bachelor-of-science/entry-requirements/) · [UniMelb: Equivalent subjects to course prerequisites](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/how-to-apply/undergraduate-study/equivalent-subjects-to-course-prerequisites)
6. **The maths prerequisite can be met without Calculus at school**, through one of three pre-approved online courses. Passing one satisfies the prerequisite on its own. *[13:21–15:55]*
   - **UniLearn Senior Mathematics:** self-paced, ~18–26 weeks, with exams; needs an overall grade of 60%+.
   - **Calculus and Probability Online** (UniMelb): ~9 weeks, or a 6-week summer intake Jan–Feb; needs a pass (50%+) in the relevant module(s). — [UniMelb: Calculus and Probability Online](https://ms.unimelb.edu.au/study/undergraduate-studies/student-resources/online-calculus-and-probability)
   - **University of Queensland Pure Mathematics** online bridging course.
   - The overview of all three: [UniMelb: Satisfy subject prerequisites](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/how-to-apply/satisfy-subject-prerequisites) · [Faculty of Science: Don't meet the prerequisites?](https://science.unimelb.edu.au/study/undergraduate-studies/science-prerequisites)
   - Staff couldn't say whether other universities accept these. *[24:41]* **Check each university.**
7. **Biomedicine vs Science.** The majors largely overlap. Public Health and Epidemiology is Biomedicine-only. Biomedicine is a smaller, more structured cohort, mostly aiming for medicine, dentistry, optometry or physiotherapy, with electives limited to the degree. Science has wider electives plus **breadth** subjects from other faculties. *[08:42–12:30]*
   - [Bachelor of Biomedicine](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/find/courses/undergraduate/bachelor-of-biomedicine/) · [Bachelor of Science structure](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/find/courses/undergraduate/bachelor-of-science/structure/) · [Biotechnology major](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/find/courses/major/biotechnology/structure/) (specialisations: agri-food, molecular, biomedical biotechnology)
8. **Course transfers** are possible after at least one semester and three completed subjects. They're assessed on the ATAR, prerequisites and first-semester results, and aren't guaranteed. *[26:51–27:43]* — [UniMelb: Transferring courses](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/how-to-apply/transferring-courses)

### Medicine (MD)

9. **The MD accepts any bachelor's degree**, even Arts. Standard entry: weighted average mark (GPA) plus GAMSAT (staff said 50/50) to be shortlisted for the MMI, then offers based on the MMI. *[03:42–05:19]* — [Doctor of Medicine entry requirements](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/find/courses/graduate/doctor-of-medicine/entry-requirements/)
   - Note: our existing data has an overall selection weighting of Interview 50% / GPA 25% / GAMSAT 25% from the 2026 handbook. Both can be true (shortlisting vs final ranking); confirm.
10. **No school-leaver route for NCEA students.** The conditional MD guarantee (Melbourne Chancellor's Scholarship) needs an ATAR of 99.90+ from **Australian Year 12 or the IB undertaken in Australia**. *[06:18–06:43]* — [Melbourne Chancellor's Scholarship](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/featured/melbourne-chancellors-scholarship) · [Graduate course guarantees](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/graduate-course-guarantees)
11. **After the MD:** provisional registration, then a one-year hospital internship. Staff couldn't say how this works for NZ. *[07:56–08:32]*
    - The Medical Council of NZ recognises Australian medical-school primary qualifications. — [MCNZ: Undergraduates / medical schools](https://www.mcnz.org.nz/registration/medical-education/undergraduatesmedical-schools/) · [MCNZ: Australian general registrants](https://www.mcnz.org.nz/registration/getting-registered/registration-pathways/general-scope/australian-general-registrants/)

### Fees

12. **Bachelor's degrees are Commonwealth supported (CSP) for NZ citizens.** Science and health subjects are mostly band 2: **$1,192 per subject, $9,537 a year** full-time (2026). The 2027 table is due around Oct–Nov. Breadth choices can move this up or down. *[16:30–17:56]* — [UniMelb: Student contribution amounts](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/how-to-apply/fees/student-contribution-amounts) · [UniMelb: Fees and payments (domestic)](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/how-to-apply/undergraduate-study/domestic-applications/fees-and-payments)
13. **Paid upfront each semester.** NZ citizens can get a CSP but generally not a HELP loan. The exception is Special Category Visa holders who have lived in Australia long-term (since childhood, 10+ years). *[16:43]* — [Study Assist: Loan eligibility — non-Australian citizens](https://www.studyassist.gov.au/loan-eligibility/non-australian-citizens) · [Dept of Education: New Zealand citizens](https://www.education.gov.au/higher-education-publications/higher-education-administrative-information-providers-october-2021/48-appendices/new-zealand-citizens)
14. **Full fee** (without a CSP) would be roughly $5k per subject. *[17:56–18:08]*
15. **MD fees:** a CSP is $13,558 a year (2026), about $52k over 4 years. Graduate CSPs are limited and merit-based; less competitive applicants are offered full-fee places. *[18:09–19:35]* — [Doctor of Medicine fees](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/find/courses/graduate/doctor-of-medicine/fees/)

### Housing

16. **University Accommodation** is three self-catered, apartment-style houses. **Residential colleges** (two run by UniMelb, the rest affiliated) have three meals a day and tutoring, and take many interstate and NZ students. *[20:41–22:30]* — [UniMelb: Student accommodation](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/accommodation) · [Residential colleges FAQ](https://www.colleges.unimelb.edu.au/frequently-asked-questions/)
17. **Timing:** start around **July 2027** for a 2028 start. Colleges are largely full by September and interview shortlisted applicants. You can secure a room before a course offer, with a withdrawal date and a cancellation/refund process if no offer comes. *[22:36–24:33]*
    - Official pages say University Accommodation applications open in August and you can apply before an offer. — [How to apply for University Accommodation](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/accommodation/how-to-apply) · [Accommodation FAQs](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/accommodation/faqs)
    - Colleges run their own timelines and interviews (e.g. Ormond's 2027 round closes 23 Oct 2026). — [Ormond: How to apply](https://ormond.unimelb.edu.au/how-to-apply) · [University College: How to apply](https://www.unicol.unimelb.edu.au/why-choose-university-college/how-to-apply-or-visit-us)
