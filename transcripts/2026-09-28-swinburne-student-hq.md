### Fees

The Student Services and Amenities Fee is billed separately. *[25:09–32:12]*

### Science and medicine

16. **Bachelor of Science is science-only,** with no humanities breadth, unlike UniMelb's model, and offers a smaller set of majors. **Biochemistry and Biotechnology** are BSc majors. *[1:05:20–1:06:50]*
    - The BSc majors are Applied Mathematics, Astronomy and Astrophysics, Biochemistry, Biotechnology, Chemistry, Environmental Science, Physics and Space Technology. 17. **Biomedical science comes two ways.** The standalone **Bachelor of Biomedical Science** has a set structure: core units, major units and electives, covering anatomy, physiology and core sciences. Alternatively, it's a major inside the more flexible **Bachelor of Health Science**. For the medicine route, the adviser pointed to these two rather than Biochemistry. *[1:06:21, 1:10:31–1:12:23]*
    - [Bachelor of Biomedical Science](https://www.swinburne.edu.au/course/undergraduate/bachelor-of-biomedical-science/) · [Health Science, Biomedical Science major](https://www.swinburne.edu.au/course/undergraduate/bachelor-of-health-science/biomedical-science/)

    - UniMelb MD needs second-year-level anatomy, physiology and biochemistry. — [UniMelb MD prerequisites](https://mdhs.unimelb.edu.au/study/prerequisites) · Deakin takes any bachelor's with GAMSAT. — [Deakin Doctor of Medicine](https://www.deakin.edu.au/course/doctor-medicine) · Monash graduate entry only takes its own approved degrees. — [Monash MD entry requirements](https://www.monash.edu/medicine/medical-school/direct-entry/domestic/entry-requirements)
