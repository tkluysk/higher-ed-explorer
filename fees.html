Tuition payments

Without HECS-HELP, the student contribution is paid upfront each semester, due around weeks 2–3 of teaching (about March and August).

    Why upfront? NZ citizens get a Commonwealth supported place (the government pays part of the cost) but generally can't borrow the rest through HECS-HELP. The exception is a Special Category Visa holder who has lived in Australia since childhood. Both the Swinburne and UniMelb advisers confirmed this.

    Band mix is an estimate. Each subject is charged by its own discipline, so electives and breadth subjects can move the total. UniMelb publishes per-subject rates once you choose subjects.

    Personal planning tool: estimates only. Contribution amounts are the 2026 government maximums; universities publish the following year's rates around October–November.
