Prices are AUD per person per week, compiled 30 Sep 2026. “From” is the cheapest advertised room, which is often a bedroom in a shared apartment or a bunk/twin room. Where a studio price was found it is listed separately. listing means the figure came from a search or aggregator listing and could not be confirmed on the operator's own page (Scape shows prices only after the page's scripts run). Rates move with availability, lease length and promotions, so confirm before booking.

Transit is door-to-door for a weekday ~8:30am departure, from PTV timetables via Transitous, including walking to and from stops. Bike and walk times are from OpenStreetMap routing. The campus is at 25 Dawson St, next to Jewell station on the Upfield line and a short walk from tram 19 on Sydney Road. Use the “Directions” link on each property for live times.
